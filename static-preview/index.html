Frequently asked questions

Does it come pre-installed with a globe?

Yes. Every COSMOS ships with the C-shaped levitation stand, the matching globe, and a power adapter for your region. Nothing else is needed.

What countries are shown on the globe?

The globe is a full political map of the world with country outlines, names and major cities printed in English.

Is there any music or sound?

No. COSMOS is completely silent. There are no speakers, fans or moving parts in the stand, just the quiet magic of magnetic levitation.

What's in the box?

1 × levitation stand with LED lighting, 1 × magnetic globe, 1 × power adapter with the plug you selected, and a quick-start guide.

How long does delivery take?

Shipping is free worldwide. UK orders typically arrive in 3–5 working days, and international orders in 7–14 working days. You'll receive a tracking link by email.

What if it stops levitating?

Simply lift the globe off, unplug the stand for 10 seconds, plug it back in and reposition the globe. If it still won't float, contact us and we'll replace it under our 30-day guarantee.

How to Set This Up

Get your floating globe ready in just a few steps.

  1. 1

    Connect & Power

    Plug in the power cable and place the stand on a flat surface.

  2. 2

    Position the Globe

    Hold the globe between the top and bottom. Slowly move it until you feel it lift and stabilise.

  3. 3

    Enjoy the Magic

    Once it floats, give it a gentle spin and enjoy your world.
